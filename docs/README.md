## Research Approach

The project uses weekly observations to track changes in @WAR's audience, music activity and online presence.

The initial research period is one month. Following the review on 12 October 2026, the project may be extended depending on the patterns identified.

## Analysis Approach

The analysis will focus on:

- Audience growth
- Cross-platform growth
- Growth acceleration
- Content performance
- Releases and collaborations
- Exposure events
- Sustained momentum

The project will avoid treating a single increase as evidence of a breakout and will instead look for patterns across time and platforms.

# UNDERGROUND-TO-BREAKOUT
> Tracking the data signals behind an underground UK rapper's rise to mainstream attention.

## Project Overview

**Underground to Breakout** is a data analytics project exploring how an emerging UK rap artist develops momentum before reaching mainstream attention.

The project tracks audience growth, content performance, music activity and significant exposure events over time to identify measurable signals that may indicate an emerging breakout.

The initial tracking period runs from **12 September 2026 to 12 October 2026**, with data collected weekly.

The project focuses on **@WAR / everybodyatwar**, a UK underground rap twin duo.

## Research Question

> What data signals can indicate that an underground UK rapper is gaining momentum before they break into the mainstream?

The project focuses on identifying patterns in audience growth, content performance and exposure over time rather than relying on a single measure of popularity.

## Why This Project?

Music, culture and social media generate large amounts of data, but an artist's growth is often discussed through popularity rather than measurable patterns.

This project combines my interests in **music, UK rap, culture and data analytics** to explore how an emerging artist's development can be tracked using publicly available data.

Rather than asking whether an artist is already successful, the project asks whether changes in their audience, content performance and exposure can provide early indicators of growing momentum.

## Methodology

The project uses a repeated weekly data collection approach to track changes in @WAR's audience and online activity.

### Data Collection
- Establish a baseline on 12 September 2026.
- Collect the same core metrics weekly.
- Record data from publicly available platform profiles.
- Log relevant releases, collaborations and exposure events.
- Record the source and date for each observation.

### Analysis
The data will be compared across collection periods to identify:
- Audience growth
- Cross-platform growth
- Growth acceleration
- Significant content or audience spikes
- Potential effects of releases, collaborations and exposure
- Whether growth is sustained over multiple weeks

Percentage change will be used to compare growth between collection periods, while indexed values may be used when comparing trends across platforms with different scales.

The initial tracking period covers **12 September 2026 – 12 October 2026**.

## Analysis

The analysis will focus on identifying patterns rather than treating any single metric as evidence of a breakout.

Key questions include:

1. Which platform shows the fastest audience growth?
2. Is growth consistent or does it occur in sudden spikes?
3. Do releases or collaborations coincide with increases in audience growth?
4. Does growth on one platform appear before another?
5. Does increased content engagement occur alongside audience growth?
6. What patterns could indicate movement from underground attention toward mainstream visibility?

The analysis will compare weekly observations and consider changes across multiple platforms and significant events.

## Limitations

This project relies on publicly available platform data, which creates several limitations:

- Some metrics may be rounded or abbreviated by platforms.
- Some engagement metrics are not publicly visible.
- Platform metrics can change between collection dates.
- Some content dates may be displayed relatively rather than as exact dates.
- Publicly available data does not represent an artist's complete audience or activity.
- Different platforms measure and display metrics differently.
- A short tracking period may not capture longer-term trends.
- Correlation between an event and audience growth does not necessarily mean that the event caused the growth.

These limitations will be considered when interpreting the results and making conclusions.
